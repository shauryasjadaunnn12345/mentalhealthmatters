# 🧠 MentalHealthMatters

MentalHealthMatters is a comprehensive psychological assessment platform designed to help individuals better understand their mental and emotional well-being through a wide range of scientifically inspired self-assessment tools.

The platform provides psychological tests for:

- Anxiety
- Depression
- Stress
- ADHD
- Burnout
- Personality
- Emotional Intelligence
- Sleep Health
- Emotional Wellness

Users can take assessments, receive instant results and AI-assisted insights, track emotional trends over time, and gain a deeper understanding of their mental health in a secure and accessible environment.

MentalHealthMatters aims to promote:

- Mental health awareness
- Early emotional screening
- Self-reflection
- Emotional wellness tracking
- Accessible psychological support

The platform is built with a focus on privacy, accessibility, and user-friendly mental health experiences using modern web technologies and AI-assisted wellness insights.
