## 📌 Overview

**MentalHealthMatters** is a psychological assessment platform designed to help individuals better understand their mental and emotional well-being through a range of scientifically-inspired self-assessment tools.

Users take assessments, receive instant results with AI-assisted insights, track emotional trends over time, and build a clearer picture of their mental health — all in a secure, accessible environment.

---

## 🧪 Assessments Offered

| Category | Focus |
|---|---|
| 😟 **Anxiety** | Screens for anxiety symptoms and severity |
| 💭 **Depression** | Screens for depressive symptoms and mood patterns |
| ⚡ **Stress** | Measures perceived stress levels |
| 🎯 **ADHD** | Screens for attention and focus-related patterns |
| 🔥 **Burnout** | Assesses signs of occupational or emotional burnout |
| 🧬 **Personality** | Explores personality traits and tendencies |
| ❤️ **Emotional Intelligence** | Measures self-awareness and emotional regulation |
| 😴 **Sleep Health** | Evaluates sleep quality and patterns |
| 🌤️ **Emotional Wellness** | General emotional well-being check-in |

## ✨ Features

- 📝 **Scientifically-Inspired Assessments** — a wide range of self-report tools across mental and emotional health domains
- ⚡ **Instant Results** — get your scores and interpretation immediately after completing an assessment
- 🤖 **AI-Assisted Insights** — personalized, AI-generated context around your results
- 📈 **Emotional Trend Tracking** — monitor how your mental health shifts over time
- 🔒 **Secure & Private by Design** — built with user privacy as a first-class concern
- ♿ **Accessible Experience** — designed to be easy to use for anyone, regardless of background with mental health tools

## 🎯 Mission

MentalHealthMatters aims to promote:

- Mental health awareness
- Early emotional screening
- Self-reflection
- Emotional wellness tracking
- Accessible psychological support

The platform is built with a focus on **privacy, accessibility, and user-friendly mental health experiences**, using modern web technologies and AI-assisted wellness insights.

---

## ⚠️ A Note on Scope

MentalHealthMatters is a **self-reflection and screening tool**, not a diagnostic or clinical instrument. Results are meant to support awareness and early self-understanding — they are not a substitute for evaluation, diagnosis, or care from a licensed mental health professional. Users experiencing significant distress should be encouraged to reach out to a qualified professional or local crisis service.
